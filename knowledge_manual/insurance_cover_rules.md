# AfyaPlus Insurance Cover Rules

> TRAINING EXAMPLE, NOT MEDICAL OR INSURANCE ADVICE. AfyaPlus Health is a fictional company
> created for a student capstone project. All plans, limits and rules below are made up.

## 1. Plans and annual outpatient limits

| Plan   | Annual outpatient limit | Annual inpatient limit | Co-payment per outpatient visit |
|--------|-------------------------|------------------------|---------------------------------|
| Bronze | KES 30,000              | KES 300,000            | KES 500                         |
| Silver | KES 75,000              | KES 1,000,000          | KES 300                         |
| Gold   | KES 150,000             | KES 3,000,000          | KES 0 (no co-payment)           |

All plans cover the principal member, one spouse and up to four children under 18 years.

## 2. Waiting periods

- General illness: 30 days from the policy start date.
- Pre-existing and chronic conditions (e.g. diabetes, hypertension, asthma): 12 months.
- Maternity: 10 months. Maternity is only available on Silver and Gold plans.
- Accidents and emergencies: no waiting period.

## 3. What is covered

- Outpatient consultations, laboratory tests and prescribed generic medicines at AfyaPlus
  partner clinics, up to the plan's outpatient limit.
- Inpatient admission, surgery and ward charges, up to the inpatient limit.
- Emergency ambulance evacuation within Kenya on all plans.
- Dental and optical care: Gold plan only, capped at KES 20,000 per year combined.

## 4. Exclusions (never covered)

- Cosmetic or aesthetic procedures, including teeth whitening.
- Treatment received outside Kenya, except emergencies on the Gold plan.
- Experimental treatments and drugs not approved by the Pharmacy and Poisons Board.
- Injuries from participation in professional sports.

## 5. Pre-authorisation

- Any inpatient admission, MRI or CT scan requires pre-authorisation from the AfyaPlus
  claims desk BEFORE the service, except in a life-threatening emergency.
- Emergency admissions must be notified to the claims desk within 48 hours.
- Claims without required pre-authorisation are paid at 50% of the approved amount.

## 6. Claims submission

- Members must submit outpatient reimbursement claims within 90 days of the visit.
- A valid claim includes the claim form, original receipts and the clinician's diagnosis.
