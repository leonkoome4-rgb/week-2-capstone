# AfyaPlus Clinic Referral and Triage Rules

> TRAINING EXAMPLE, NOT MEDICAL ADVICE. AfyaPlus Health is a fictional company created for a
> student capstone project. These routing rules are simplified and made up.

## 1. Care levels

- **Level 1 - Telehealth nurse line:** minor complaints such as a mild cold, mild headache or
  medication refill questions.
- **Level 2 - Partner outpatient clinic:** fever lasting more than 48 hours, ear infections,
  urinary symptoms, minor cuts that need dressing, routine child immunisations.
- **Level 3 - Referral hospital:** problems that need a specialist, imaging (MRI/CT) or
  admission.

## 2. Emergency red flags - go to the nearest emergency department immediately

Route the patient straight to emergency care (no referral letter needed) if any of these occur:

- Chest pain or difficulty breathing.
- Signs of stroke: face drooping, arm weakness, slurred speech.
- Fever above 39°C in a baby younger than 3 months.
- A child with fever who has a seizure, is unusually drowsy, or cannot drink.
- Heavy bleeding that does not stop with pressure.
- Pregnancy with severe abdominal pain or bleeding.

## 3. Specialist referral rules

- A specialist visit (paediatrician, cardiologist, gynaecologist, etc.) is covered only with
  a referral letter from an AfyaPlus partner clinic doctor.
- Referral letters are valid for 30 days from the date of issue.
- Self-referral to a specialist is reimbursed at 50% of the approved amount.

## 4. Chronic disease programme

- Members with diabetes or hypertension are enrolled in the chronic care programme after
  their 12-month waiting period.
- Programme members must review with a partner clinic doctor every 3 months to keep
  medication cover active.

## 5. Paediatric routing

- Children under 5 years with fever should be seen at a Level 2 clinic within 24 hours.
- Weight must be recorded in kilograms at every paediatric visit because medication doses
  are weight-based.
